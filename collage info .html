Admission Process

Follow these steps to complete the admission process:

  1. Choose your preferred course.
  2. Check the eligibility requirements.
  3. Fill out the admission application form.
  4. Submit the required documents.
  5. Complete the admission fee payment.
  6. Receive admission confirmation from the University.
